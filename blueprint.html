About

Paper to
production.

I started out designing pixels for clients. I now lead the teams that ship them, at enterprise scale.

My first venture, PicAffectz, was a web development and graphic design studio I ran while still studying IT. It taught me early that a good interface is a promise kept: what the client sees on paper has to work in the browser.

Since then I've built for consultancy, healthcare and insurance. At Deloitte I spent nearly four years shipping web products. At Optum, UnitedHealth Group I sharpened front-end engineering at real scale. Today at Bandhan Life (formerly Aegon Life) I lead engineering, taking requirements from document to dependable software.

I care about the details users feel and the decisions teams can stand behind: clean architecture, typed code, honest estimates and interfaces that make complex numbers easy to read.
